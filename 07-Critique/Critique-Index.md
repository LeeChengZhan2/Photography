precisely what a picture does, then build a set that says one thing.

## Assignments
A7.1 twenty critiques · A7.2 cold self-critique · A7.3 steal deliberately ·
A7.4 sequence twelve from thirty

**A7.1 is the input.** Twenty careful critiques of other people's work — half of it work
you dislike — teach you more about your own pictures than another month of shooting.
