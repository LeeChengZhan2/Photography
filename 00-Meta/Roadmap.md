# Roadmap — 28 Weeks

The [[Syllabus]] is *what*. This is *when*. Dates assume a start of **Mon 3 Aug 2026**
and a budget of **~5 hours per week**. Shift the dates freely; keep the sequence.

> [!tip] The sequence is not arbitrary
> Exposure before light, light before composition, composition before post. Each
> module's assignments assume the previous module's control. Reordering is the most
> common way self-taught photographers stall.

---

## Weekly rhythm (~5 h)

| Slot | Time | What |
|---|---|---|
| **Read & note** | 1 h | Write 1–2 concept notes from the module list. Your own words. |
| **Shoot** | 2 h | The week's assignment. To brief, not to mood. |
| **Edit & cull** | 1 h | Ingest, cull, develop the keepers. |
| **Review** | 30 min | A [[Shoot-Log]] entry: what you tried, what failed, what's next. |
| **Look at others' work** | 30 min | One [[Critique]] note. Non-negotiable — this is the input side. |

The shoot slot is the one that gets skipped. Protect it: put it in the calendar with a
location, not just a time.

## Variants

**Fast track (14 weeks, ~10 h/week).** Halve every module, keep every gate, drop
Module 5 to one genre. Do not compress Module 1 — double the shooting instead.

**Slow burn (52 weeks, ~2–3 h/week).** One assignment per week rather than per
module-week. Gates stay in the same order; dates roughly double.

**Trip-driven.** If a trip lands mid-syllabus, pull the relevant genre note forward
from Module 5 and treat the trip as that module's ten-frame set. Then return to
sequence. Don't try to learn exposure *and* a new place at the same time.

---

## When you fall behind

You will. The failure mode isn't slipping a week — it's abandoning the sequence
because you slipped. Two rules:

1. **Never skip a shoot slot to catch up on reading.** Notes without frames are
   trivia. Frames without notes still teach you something.
2. **Slip the dates, not the gates.** Move the whole calendar right. The gates exist
   because the later modules genuinely don't work without the earlier ones.

Log the slip in the week's [[Shoot-Log]] with the reason. After eight weeks you'll see
the real pattern — usually a time of day or a location that never works — and can
design around it.
